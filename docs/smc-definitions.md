# Smart money concept definitions used by Kestrel

These are one fixed, mechanical reading of concepts that are discretionary in their origin. Different readings give different trades, which is why the rules are written down and the detectors are tested against them (`crates/fx-core/src/detect/mod.rs`). Nothing here claims the concepts are profitable.

## The causality rule
`known_at` is the index of the candle whose close makes the event knowable. Events are returned sorted by `known_at` (stable, then by emission order). For every n in 1..=len, `analyze(&c[..n])` must equal the events of `analyze(&c)` filtered to `known_at < n`. Write a test that does this for every n over several hundred candles of deterministic pseudo-random data (a small xorshift generator inside the test, fixed seeds, three seeds) and also for hand-made trending and ranging series. This test is the most important deliverable.

## Definitions
Let `L = cfg.swing_len`.
1. Swing high at bar i: `high[i]` is strictly greater than the highs of the L bars before and the L bars after. Swing low is symmetric with lows. `known_at = i + L`. Emit `Swing`. Needs i >= L.
2. Structure state: keep the latest confirmed swing high and latest confirmed swing low (updated when they become known, i.e. at `known_at`, before evaluating breaks on that same bar). Keep a trend: `None` at start, `Bull` or `Bear` after the first break.
3. Break on bar t (evaluate every bar after updating swings): a bullish break occurs when the latest swing high has not yet been broken and `close[t] > level` (if `close_break`) or `high[t] > level` (otherwise). Bearish symmetric on the latest swing low. After a break, mark that swing level as broken so it cannot break again. If both a bullish and a bearish break occur on the same bar, emit neither (ambiguous) and mark neither.
   - A bullish break while trend is `Bull`, or while trend is `None`, is a BOS (`choch = false`). A bullish break while trend is `Bear` is a CHoCH (`choch = true`). Bearish symmetric. The first break ever (trend `None`) is a BOS. After emitting, set trend to the break direction. Emit `Break` with `level` = the swing price, `swing_idx` = that swing candle, `known_at = t`.
4. Fair value gap on bar i (i >= 2): bullish if `low[i] > high[i-2]`, zone bottom `high[i-2]`, top `low[i]`; bearish if `high[i] < low[i-2]`, zone top `low[i-2]`, bottom `high[i]`. Emit `Fvg` with a fresh `id` (shared counter with order blocks, starting at 0, increasing in emission order) and `known_at = i`. Ignore gaps smaller than 1e-9.
   - Lifecycle, evaluated from bar i+1 on: `Touched` the first bar whose range overlaps the zone (`low <= top && high >= bottom`). `Mitigated` the first bar that CLOSES beyond the far side (bullish gap: `close < bottom`; bearish gap: `close > top`). After `Mitigated`, no more events for that id. A bar may emit both Touched and Mitigated, Touched first.
5. Order block, emitted at each `Break` bar t, right after the Break event, with the same `known_at`. Bullish break: the leg origin is the bar with the lowest low in `(swing_idx, t]`; the order block candle is the latest bearish-close candle (`close < open`) with index in `[origin-4, origin]` (clamped at 0); if none, the origin candle itself. Zone is that candle's `low..high` (top = high, bottom = low). Bearish break is symmetric (origin is the highest high, look for the latest bullish-close candle). Same lifecycle as a gap (Touched on first overlap from t+1, Mitigated on a close through the far side). The block's `dir` is the break direction.
6. Sweep on bar t: bar t trades beyond a confirmed swing high (`high[t] > level`) but `close[t] <= level`, where level is the latest confirmed swing high that has not been broken by a close and not already swept. Emit `Sweep { dir: Bear, ... }` (expects reversal down). Symmetric for lows with `dir: Bull`. Mark that swing swept so it fires once. A bar that qualifies for a Break on the same level is not a sweep.
Evaluation order per bar t: confirm swings (emit Swing), update state, breaks (+ order block), sweeps, lifecycle updates of existing gaps and blocks (for bars after their formation), then new FVG for bar t.
